A commonplace book is a collection of things that catch your attention. Quotes, questions, fragments, and connections. Its value comes from the person doing the collecting.

## Notice, then return

Write down the small observation before it disappears. Give it a sentence of context. Return to it after some time has passed.

You may discover that the observation was ordinary. Or that it belongs next to something you noticed last month. Either outcome is useful.

## Leave a trail

When a thought comes from somewhere else, keep the source alongside it. Future you will appreciate knowing which part was the original idea and which part was your response.

A publication can grow out of this kind of practice: less pressure to produce, more attention to what is already there.
