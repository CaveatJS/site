Every publication begins with a small decision: this is worth writing down.

It might be a question you keep returning to. An observation from your work. A conversation that deserves more space than a message thread. You don’t need an editorial calendar to begin. You need a page.

## Make yourself at home

This is a sample essay in your new Caveat publication. Replace it with your own writing in the local editor, or open its Markdown file in `content/posts`.

The words on this page, the type they are set in, and the way the whole thing comes together are yours to change.

> Your website is the publication. Everything else is a way of finding it.

## A practice, not a performance

A useful publication can be modest. A short note each week. A record of what changed your mind. A place where unfinished questions can become better ones.

Start with something specific. Write it plainly. Leave room for a second thought.

We’ll be here when you’re ready.
